Magic Sort - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

RD Projects ("we", "our", or "us") developed and operates Magic Sort ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Magic Sort.

1. We Do Not Directly Collect Your Personal Data

Magic Sort does not require you to create an account and RD Projects does not directly collect, store, or sell your personal information through the App.

You can play Magic Sort without providing your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or other personal information directly to us.

However, third-party services integrated into the App, such as Google AdMob and Google Play, may independently process certain information as described in this Privacy Policy and according to their own privacy policies.

2. Third-Party Advertising

Magic Sort uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may process certain information to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising services.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and applicable settings, information processed by advertising services may include:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device and app information
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction information
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Diagnostic and performance information

RD Projects does not directly receive or store your personal information from AdMob for its own independent user database.

For more information about how Google handles information in advertising services, please review Google's privacy policies and advertising information.

3. Advertising ID

Google AdMob may use the Android Advertising ID or other advertising-related identifiers to support advertising and measurement.

You can manage advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google account settings. Available controls may vary depending on your Android version, device manufacturer, country, and applicable regulations.

4. Internet and Network Access

Magic Sort requires Internet access for certain features, including loading advertisements and communicating with Google Play services for in-app purchases.

The App also checks network availability to determine whether an Internet connection is available.

Magic Sort does not use the Internet connection to upload your locally stored game progress to RD Projects.

5. Notifications

Magic Sort may provide an optional daily reminder notification at approximately 5:00 PM to encourage you to continue playing.

On Android 13 and newer versions, notification permission may be requested before notifications can be displayed.

Notifications can be disabled at any time through your device settings.

6. Background Startup

Magic Sort may use the Android RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED permission to restore scheduled reminder notifications after your device restarts.

This permission is not used to collect personal information or monitor your device activity.

7. App Permissions

Magic Sort may use the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET - Used to load advertisements and communicate with required online services, including Google Play Billing.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE - Used to check whether a network connection is available.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS - Used to display optional daily reminder notifications on supported Android versions. Permission is requested at runtime where required.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED - Used to restore scheduled daily reminder notifications after the device restarts.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING - Used to enable optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.

Magic Sort does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, phone calls, SMS, or personal files.

8. Game Progress and Local Storage

Your game progress, level completion, stars, coins, medals, settings, streak information, and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on your device.

This locally stored gameplay information is used to provide the game experience and is not uploaded to or stored on RD Projects servers.

If you uninstall the App or clear its application data, locally stored game progress may be deleted.

9. In-App Purchases

Magic Sort may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.

Purchases are processed through Google Play. Payment information, such as complete credit card numbers, debit card numbers, or banking credentials, is handled by Google Play and is not collected or stored by RD Projects.

Google Play may process information necessary to complete and manage purchases, verify transactions, provide purchase history, and support refunds or other transaction-related services.

For information about Google's handling of purchase-related information, please review the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

10. Third-Party Services

Magic Sort uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

  • Google AdMob - Used to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.
  • Google Play Billing - Used to process optional in-app purchases.

These third-party services operate independently from RD Projects and may collect or process information according to their own policies.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Since RD Projects does not maintain a user account system or a personal information database for Magic Sort, we do not maintain a separate database of your personal information.

12. Children's Privacy

Magic Sort is a family-friendly puzzle game and does not require users to create an account or submit personal information directly to RD Projects.

Magic Sort does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Third-party services such as Google AdMob may have their own policies, age requirements, consent mechanisms, and child-directed treatment. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

13. Your Choices

You can control several aspects of your experience with Magic Sort:

  • Disable notifications through your Android device settings.
  • Manage advertising and privacy settings through Android and Google settings.
  • Choose whether to make optional in-app purchases.
  • Uninstall the App at any time.

14. Data Retention

RD Projects does not maintain a personal user account database for Magic Sort. Game progress and settings stored locally on your device remain there until they are removed by the App, cleared through device settings, or deleted when the App is uninstalled, subject to Android system behavior.

Information processed by third-party services is retained according to their respective policies and practices.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Magic Sort, third-party services, legal requirements, or our privacy practices.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

16. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or Magic Sort, please contact us at:

Email: rdprojects24@gmail.com

Magic Sort
