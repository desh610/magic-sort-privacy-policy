Magic Sort Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Magic Sort by RD Projects
Last updated: September 2, 2026

RD Projects ("we", "our", "us", or "RD Projects") developed and operates Magic Sort (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download or use Magic Sort.

1. We Do Not Collect Your Personal Data

Magic Sort does not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

You do not need to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, videos, microphone recordings, or location to use the App.

RD Projects does not operate its own servers for collecting or storing your personal information through Magic Sort.

Your game progress, settings, and other gameplay-related information are stored locally on your device and are not sent to RD Projects.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

2. Third-Party Advertising

Magic Sort uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

While RD Projects does not directly collect your personal data, Google AdMob may independently collect or process certain information through its advertising technology.

Depending on your device settings, consent choices, region, and applicable laws, this may include information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device and app information
  • IP address
  • Information related to advertisements and ad interactions
  • Information used for advertising measurement and personalization

This processing is performed by Google and is subject to Google's own privacy policies, terms, and applicable consent mechanisms.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

Google Privacy Policy

You can also learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Advertising ID

Google AdMob and other advertising technologies may use the Android Advertising ID or other advertising-related identifiers to provide and measure advertisements.

You can manage or reset your Android Advertising ID through your Android device settings. The available options may vary depending on your Android version and device manufacturer.

4. Internet and Network Access

Magic Sort uses Internet access primarily for network-dependent functionality, including loading advertisements provided through Google AdMob.

The core puzzle gameplay is designed to work offline and does not require an Internet connection.

The App may also check the device's network connectivity before performing network-dependent operations.

5. Notifications

Magic Sort may optionally send a daily reminder notification to help you maintain your daily play streak.

On Android 13 and later, the App requests notification permission before sending notifications.

Notifications are optional. You can allow, deny, or disable notifications at any time through your Android device settings.

6. Background Startup

Magic Sort may use Android's RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED permission to restore the optional daily reminder schedule after the device restarts.

This permission is used only to maintain the notification reminder functionality and is not used to collect personal information.

7. App Permissions

Magic Sort uses the following Android permissions:

INTERNET Used to load advertisements through Google AdMob and support network-dependent functionality.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used to determine whether the device has an available network connection before performing network-dependent operations.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Used for the optional daily reminder notification on Android 13 and later. The user can deny or disable this permission.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Used to re-schedule the optional daily reminder after the device restarts.

Magic Sort does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone calls, SMS messages, or personal files.

Vibration and haptic feedback are provided using Android/Jetpack Compose haptic feedback APIs and do not require the VIBRATE permission.

8. Game Progress and Local Storage

Magic Sort stores gameplay information locally on your device to provide features such as game progress, level completion, stars, coins, medals, daily streaks, settings, and other game preferences.

This locally stored information is not uploaded to RD Projects' servers.

If you uninstall the App or clear its local application data, locally stored game information may be deleted.

9. In-App Purchases

If Magic Sort provides in-app purchases through Google Play Billing, payment processing is handled by Google Play.

RD Projects does not receive or store your complete payment card number, banking credentials, or other complete payment credentials through the App.

Google Play's handling of payment and transaction information is subject to Google's applicable policies and terms.

10. Third-Party Services

Magic Sort may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying and measuring advertisements.
  • Google Play — for app distribution and, where applicable, in-app purchase processing.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies. RD Projects does not control how these third-party services process information.

11. Data Security

Because Magic Sort does not collect or maintain personal information on RD Projects' own servers, there is no personal user database maintained by RD Projects for the App.

However, third-party services such as Google AdMob and Google Play operate their own systems and security measures. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

12. Children's Privacy

Magic Sort is designed to be family-friendly and does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to RD Projects.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party advertising services may have their own policies, safeguards, and requirements regarding children and age-restricted users.

13. Your Choices

Depending on your Android device, location, and applicable laws, you may be able to:

  • Manage or reset your advertising identifier.
  • Manage notification permissions.
  • Manage personalized advertising settings.
  • Control app permissions through Android settings.
  • Manage available advertising consent options.

Changing certain settings may affect the availability or personalization of advertisements.

14. Data Retention

RD Projects does not collect or maintain personal information through Magic Sort and therefore does not retain a personal user database.

Gameplay information stored locally on your device remains there until it is removed by you, the App, or the Android operating system.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies and retention practices.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, third-party services, legal requirements, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
