# Privacy Policy for Magic Sort **Last updated: September 2, 2026** RD Projects ("we", "our", or "us") developed and operates **Magic Sort** (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Magic Sort. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. ## 1. Information We Collect Magic Sort is designed to provide a simple and privacy-conscious puzzle game experience. The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, microphone recordings, or camera data. Game progress and gameplay-related information may be stored locally on your device so that you can continue playing. ## 2. Advertising Magic Sort uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process certain information automatically, such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and information about ad interactions, depending on the user's device settings, consent choices, and applicable laws. This information may be used to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising services. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms. For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy You can also learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites ## 3. Advertising ID The advertising services used by the App may use the Android Advertising ID or other advertising-related identifiers. You can manage or reset your Android Advertising ID through your device settings. The exact options may vary depending on your Android version and device manufacturer. ## 4. Internet and Network Access The App uses Internet access primarily to: * Load advertisements provided through Google AdMob. * Support network-dependent functionality when required. * Check network connectivity. The core puzzle gameplay is designed to work offline. ## 5. Notifications Magic Sort may optionally send a daily reminder notification to help you maintain your daily play streak. On Android 13 and later, the App requests notification permission before sending notifications. You can allow or disable notifications at any time through your device settings. ## 6. Background Startup The App may use Android's `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` permission to restore the notification reminder schedule after the device restarts. This permission is used only for maintaining the optional daily reminder functionality. ## 7. App Permissions Magic Sort may request or use the following permissions: ### INTERNET Used to load advertisements and support network-dependent functionality. ### ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used to determine whether the device has an available network connection before performing network-dependent operations. ### POST_NOTIFICATIONS Used for the optional daily reminder notification on Android 13 and later. The user can deny or disable this permission. ### RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Used to re-schedule the optional daily reminder after the device restarts. The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone calls, SMS, or personal files. ## 8. In-App Purchases If Magic Sort provides purchases through Google Play Billing, payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your complete payment card or other payment credentials. Google Play's handling of payment and transaction information is subject to Google's applicable policies. ## 9. Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 10. Children's Privacy Magic Sort does not knowingly request personal information from children. The App does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to us. Advertising services may have their own policies and requirements regarding children and age-restricted users. ## 11. Third-Party Services Magic Sort uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies. These services may include: * **Google AdMob** — advertising and related measurement services. * **Google Play** — app distribution and, where applicable, in-app purchase processing. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these third-party services for more information about how they process information. ## 12. Your Choices Depending on your device and location, you may have options to: * Disable or reset your advertising identifier. * Manage notification permissions. * Manage personalized advertising settings. * Control app permissions through Android settings. * Disable or restrict certain advertising technologies through available device or consent settings. Some settings may affect the availability or personalization of advertisements. ## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, third-party services, legal requirements, or our privacy practices. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. ## 14.
